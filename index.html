House rules in force. Decks: players + 2. Deal 11 to the hand, 11 to the foot. The stock is split into four draw piles — drawing two means one card from each of two different piles. Or take the discard pile with two naturals matching the top card, and you get that card plus the six behind it. To go down the first time you need at least 50 / 90 / 120 / 150 across the four rounds — totalled over every meld you lay in that one turn, not a single meld and not an exact figure. Books of seven: red book all naturals, 500; black book with wilds, 300. Threes never meld — a black three on top freezes the pile, and a red three is dead weight you discard like any other card: it costs you 100 only if you are still holding one when the round ends. Go out from your foot with a red book and a black book, discarding your last card. +100 for going out.
