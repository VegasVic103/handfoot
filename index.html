House rules in force. Decks: players + 2. Deal 11 to the hand, 11 to the foot. The stock is split into four draw piles — drawing two means one card from each of two different piles. Or take the discard pile with two naturals matching the top card, and you get that card plus the six behind it. To go down the first time you need at least 50 / 90 / 120 / 150 across the four rounds — totalled over every meld you lay in that one turn, not a single meld and not an exact figure. A pile take counts towards that total like anything else, and if you cannot reach it you hand the pile back. Books of seven: red book all naturals, 500; black book with wilds, 300. Seven closes a book rather than capping it — keep adding to the pile, or start a second book of that rank. Threes never meld — a black three on top freezes the pile, and a red three is dead weight you discard like any other card: it costs you 100 only if you are still holding one when the round ends. To go out you must be in your foot with a red book and a black book, and then play every card left in your hand into melds — there is no final discard. +500 for going out.
